# Future You Planner

Prototype of a mobile app called 'Future You', it is a financial planning app, create a bare bones but stylish template, the app has sections for: "Converse", "Advantage", "Profile". In the Converse section, present a screen that acts as a chatbot, at the top of the chatbot screen is a AI depicted person to give a visual model to the chatbot response, the AI depicted person should look like a 60 year old lebanese guy, the default chatbot message should suggest starting prompts including 'How can I grow my business?', 'Tell me about small business support programs', 'Help me with services beyond financial planning'. The Advantage page has a top section with a horizontal tinder style swipe view of "Available Benefits" including 'Toll Relief', 'Safety Equipment Rebates', 'Wage Subsidies', 'Carbon Credit Scheme', 'Electric Vehicles Incentives' and each of these swipe boxes has a relevant picture shown. At the bottom half of the Advantage page, show navigation options for 'My Benefits Submissions'. When 'My Benefits Submission' is selected it opens a page showing a list of submitted benefits, such as 'EV Stamp Duty Waiver' and 'Reservists Employee Scheme'. When a benefit is selected it shows a summary of the submission progress, a link to more information, and other information
